## A complete example

> One realistic case, shown end to end: the situation, what was written or run,
> and what came out. Show the actual artifact — not a description of it.
>
> State the problem before the solution. A reader who has tried to predict the
> answer learns from the difference; one who is handed it only recognises it.

## A variation

> The same case with one constraint changed, and what had to change in response.
> Name the constraint and point at the difference — do not re-present the whole
> example.
>
> This is what turns an example into a pattern. One case shows that the tool
> works; two that differ in one respect show which part was doing the work.

Replace this paragraph.

## A case that does not fit

> An example where the tool is the wrong choice, shown as concretely as the ones
> where it is right. What was tried, how it went badly, and what to use instead.
>
> This must agree with § When to reach for it. That section states the bounds;
> this one shows a reader what crossing them looks like.
