## Example: a contrasting case

> A second situation, chosen to differ from the first in a way that changes what
> the reader does. Say what changed and what stayed the same — the difference is
> the lesson, and an example that differs only in its nouns teaches nothing new.
>
> The most useful contrast is often a case near the edge of § When to reach for
