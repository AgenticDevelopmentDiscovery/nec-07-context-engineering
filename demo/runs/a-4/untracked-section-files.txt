## A complete example

> One case, start to finish, as it would actually be done. Show the real input,
> the real commands or code, and the real result — not a description of them.
>
> Do not re-teach the steps; § Content already did. Say only what a reader could
> not work out from there: why this case, and what to look at in the result.

## A contrasting example

> A second case chosen because it differs from the first in a way that matters —
> a different setting, scale, or constraint. Name the difference up front, so
> the reader knows what to compare.
>
> Two examples that differ only in surface detail teach one example. If you
> cannot say what the contrast is in a sentence, pick a different case.
