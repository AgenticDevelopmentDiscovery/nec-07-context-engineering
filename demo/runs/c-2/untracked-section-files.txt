## The same tool, a different setting

> A second task that looks different on the surface and is solved the same way.
> Say what changed from the first example and what stayed the same — the part
> that stayed the same is what the reader should carry away.
>
> Choose the contrast deliberately. Two examples that differ only in their data
> teach one example twice; two that differ in setting teach the tool.
