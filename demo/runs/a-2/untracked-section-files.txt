## A contrasting example

> A second task, chosen because it differs from the first in a way that matters
> — a different scale, a different constraint, a different outcome. Say what the
> difference is before you show it.
>
> Two examples that vary one thing teach the reader what that thing does. Two
> that vary nothing teach the same lesson twice; cut one.
