## A contrasting example

> A second whole case, chosen to differ from the first in the way that matters
> most — a different scale, setting, or constraint. Say what changed and what
> stayed the same.
>
> One example reads as a recipe; two let the reader see which parts belong to
> the tool and which belonged to the case. Pick the contrast deliberately: a
> second example that varies nothing important teaches nothing new.
