## A contrasting example

> A second case chosen because it differs from the first in a way that matters
> — another setting, another scale, another constraint. Say what changed and
> what the tool did differently because of it.
>
> Two examples that vary along one axis teach the axis. Two that are
> interchangeable teach nothing the first did not, so cut one.
