# Clarity & Exposition — Round 3

**Recommendation:** minor polish
the arc and the hedging are sound, but the tutorial's headline claim (the index) and its central term are stated only in notes, and a handful of labels contradict the text beneath them

## Strengths
- The through-line holds: what (two stores, one budget) → why (the loop fills the window) → how (read it, test it, index it, layer it, defer it, diagnose it) → what else (limits and onward pointers). The roadmap sentence in § What this tutorial covers names the route in the order the sections then take it, and the four capabilities return word for word in § What you can do now with the unit that delivered each. A reader can check the promise against the delivery without effort.
- The "rewording" motif is planted once and paid off once. § Why agents make the window the bottleneck says rewording "cannot supply information the model cannot see", then concedes the exception ("A prompt that names a file is different: it changes the window"); § Pitfalls closes on "Every fix changes what is loaded, or when. None is a rewording." The exception is what makes the claim precise rather than a slogan.
- Claims are sized to their evidence, in plain words: "that is advice, not evidence", "a vendor report", "We infer, and did not measure", "Post hoc, not pre-registered", "none of these effects is tested in agentic coding in any source we found". The null result is reported as a null result and the failed prediction is named. This costs a few clauses and buys the reader's trust in everything else.
- *Compaction* is defined at first use in one sentence and then used consistently (§ The window is a budget you can read, § CLAUDE.md as an index, § Pitfalls). "On demand" is held as the term throughout, with "just in time" appearing once and attributed.
- § Layers and § Pitfalls are terse and lose nothing by it. "The fix for a conflict is to remove it, not to predict the winner" is the whole lesson in one line.
- The spines are in note form, tag their sources, and record rejected alternatives with reasons. They are not accumulating unrealised intentions: nearly every claim is either in the prose or marked "not in the prose" with a reason.

## Weaknesses
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § What changed, and what did not: the closing paragraph, the only prose on that slide, speaks only of arm B ("The manual changed none of the six scored behaviours…"). Arm C, the arm that tests the tutorial's thesis, is never put into words outside a notes bullet one section later ("The index matched the manual at the lowest cost of the three"). On the slides the index result exists only as a table row the audience must interpret alone.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § What changed: "Tokens that change no scored behaviour are cost on any window" answers a question the prose never asks. The spine knows the question ("5k tokens on a 1.0M window, why bother?"); the reader does not, so "on any window" reads as filler rather than as "however large the window".
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § CLAUDE.md as an index, not a manual: "index" is in the title of capability 2 and of this unit, but its definition ("An index holds commands, conventions that cannot be inferred from the files, and pointers") is in a notes block, after the excerpt. On the slides the term is never defined; in the document the example precedes the definition.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § The demo: one prompt, two windows: the heading says two and the **Arms** bullet beneath it lists three. The spine's reason for keeping the heading ("This unit compares A with B. C is reported under § CLAUDE.md as an index") no longer matches the prose, which introduces C here and reports it in the table under § What changed.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § The demo, **Observables**: "2 the sidecar in note form; 3 numeric order" use the template's private vocabulary. "Sidecar" and "note form" are defined nowhere in the shipped text, and "numeric order" does not say order of what. The reader cannot tell what was scored, and therefore cannot weigh "5 of 5".
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § What changed, table: "Observables 1–5 | 5 of 5" is ambiguous because there are five observables and five runs. The caption ("Runs meeting each observable, of five") resolves it only on a careful second reading.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § What changed, notes, **Why**: "Among the repository's files, observable 5 is stated only in `CLAUDE.md`; no run committed, in any arm." The bullet is headed "Why" and explains observables 1 to 4, then states a fact about 5 that contradicts the explanation and leaves it there. Arm A had no `CLAUDE.md` and did not commit; the reader is handed a puzzle with no acknowledgement that it is one.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § On demand: the label "**Reachable only.**" is contradicted by its own bullet, which ends "its skills, commands and subagents are [loaded], so it can still spend budget at launch". `topic.md` has the accurate description: `--add-dir` "belongs to neither group".
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § On demand, **Front-loaded**: the list names only `CLAUDE.md` and its imports. Skill descriptions and auto memory, which § The window is a budget you can read says load "before you type anything", appear only by example under "**Here.**". The two lists the unit contrasts are not complete on the front-loaded side.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § Layers: "managed policy, user, project, local" are named and never located. The reader is asked to "place an instruction in the right layer" without being told which file each layer is. `CLAUDE.local.md` appears in the second bullet unintroduced.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § What `/context` shows at launch: the caption leads with "32.5k spent", a number that is not in the figure (the notes concede the total is in the header of the full readout, outside the crop). The reader looks for it in the table and does not find it.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § Pitfalls: "a superseded run" and "a dry run" are names from the demo's own record. The reader has met neither.
- `sections/02-motivation.prose.md` — § How a window fails, **Too little**: "0 of 15 runs" arrives before the reader knows there are three arms of five, and "the template's own sections" before the template has been introduced. The only signpost forward ("§ The demo") is in the notes block, so it is absent from the slide; and that notes sentence ("In the demo's control, the agent followed what was in the window over a rule that was not") restates the bullet using "control", a term not defined until § The demo.
- `sections/02-motivation.prose.md` — § Why agents make the window the bottleneck: "The name is recent. 'Context engineering' gained currency in June 2025…" connects to nothing before or after it. The spine says the origin is folded in because "it explains why the problem changed when agents became multi-step"; the sentence as written explains nothing of the kind.
- `sections/02-motivation.prose.md` — § How a window fails: "**The bound.**" is an opaque label for a limit on the diagnosis, sitting in a list of failure modes it is not one of. "spacing biases" is used without a gloss.
- `sections/01-context.prose.md`, `sections/02-motivation.prose.md`, `sections/03-content.prose.md` — one object carries four names: "this repository" (roadmap, figure notes), "the template" (§ How a window fails), "the substrate" (§ The demo), "this template" (§ CLAUDE.md as an index, notes). The `/context` figure is read from the repository as it is now and the demo from the template commit `b9f2176`; the drifting names hide that these are different states.
- `sections/03-content.concepts.md` — § The demo is stale against the prose (C's placement and the "two windows" decision, as above). § The window is a budget you can read and § What `/context` shows at launch carry "Superseded" and "round 002" history inside Decisions; that is changelog, not reasoning, and it makes the live decision harder to find.
- `sections/04-conclusion.concepts.md` — maps capability 1 to § The window is a budget you can read; the prose maps it to § What `/context` shows at launch. `sections/01-context.concepts.md` still lists "Quote the Anthropic definition, or state our own and cite theirs as agreeing?" as open; the prose has already chosen the second.
