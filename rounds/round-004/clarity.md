# Clarity & Exposition — Round 4

**Recommendation:** needs revision
the case for the index rests on a token cost whose significance the prose never establishes against its own figure (93.5% of the window free), and three working terms are used before they are defined.

## Strengths
- The through-line is stated once and kept: § What this tutorial covers lists four capabilities and a route, § What you can do now repeats the four word for word with the unit that delivered each, and the route matches the order of the eight units in `03-content`. A reader always knows where they are.
- The null result is reported as one, and its closing sentence carries it: "On a repository that describes itself, the file changed the cost of reaching the answer, not the answer." That is the clearest sentence in the document and it sits where the reader needs it, directly under the table.
- The slide/notes split works as an economy device. Every content frame in `output/slides.pdf` holds (pp. 3–4, 6–7, 9–16, 18–19 read without crowding), and the `::: notes` blocks carry the detail that would have overflowed them. § What changed, and what did not is the best case: one table, one paragraph.
- Compaction is introduced where it is first needed (§ The window is a budget you can read) and only then used (§ CLAUDE.md as an index, § Pitfalls). "The harness, Claude Code" and "managed policy" are glossed at first use.
- Hedges are attached to the claim they qualify rather than collected in a disclaimer: each study in § How a window fails is dated and cited only for what it tested.

## Weaknesses
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — the scale question is answered without being asked. The figure shows 32.5k spent of 1.0M with "Free space 934.5k 93.5%"; the demo's saving is 41.3k against 46.6k. The only reply is "Tokens that change no scored behaviour are cost on any window", which a reader cannot decode as a reply because nothing has posed the question. The spine poses it outright ("5k tokens on a 1.0M window, why bother?") in two places; the prose never does. This sits under the tutorial's main recommendation, and under § Why agents make the window the bottleneck, whose title the figure appears to contradict.
- `sections/02-motivation.prose.md` — "Too little … (§ The demo)" sends the reader to a demo whose slides report that the arm with no file met all five conventions in 5 of 5 runs. The actual case, the control, is named only in the notes of this unit; in § What changed it appears as a column headed "Control" and the sentence "After the runs, we explain the zero by the rule being in no arm's window", which does not say that this is the too-little failure the reader was promised.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — "B, the manual" and "C, the index" are introduced in § The demo by line count alone (240 and 33). What makes an index an index is given two units later. Until then the comparison is between a long file and a short one.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — "C, the index: 33 lines, reported in the table under § What changed, and what did not." All three arms are reported in that table; the clause singles out C for no reason the reader can see.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § On demand: reachable is not loaded never defines "reachable". The body classifies by "front-loaded" and "on demand"; the heading and § Pitfalls classify by "reachable" and "loaded". Whether "on demand" and "reachable" are the same thing is left to the reader. The `--add-dir` bullet is labelled "Both"; `topic.md` § Scope says it "belongs to neither group".
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § Pitfalls, third bullet: "a superseded run recovered a deleted `CLAUDE.md` from git history, and a dry run acted on an auto memory saved in another directory." "Superseded run" and "dry run" are the team's record-keeping terms; neither is introduced anywhere in the prose.
- `sections/02-motivation.prose.md` — "alone and unconflicted" in **The bound**. The spine explains what "alone" is for (a rule buried in a file that is too long is still a context problem). The prose does not, and nothing in a window is literally alone.

- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — § What changed, notes, **Why**: six sentences, of which the last ("the demo's command did not turn them off, and the page does not say what they tell the agent about when to commit") is a disclosure with no conclusion drawn.
