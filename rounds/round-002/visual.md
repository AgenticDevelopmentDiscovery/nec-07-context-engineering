## Strengths
- Presentation: all 19 pages of `output/slides.pdf` were read. 14 content frames, one title, four section dividers (pp. 2, 5, 8, 17), and no second divider for any section, so nothing sits between a `#` and its first `##`. The 2, 2, 8, 2 split is what was built.
- Presentation: the `::: notes` convention does what it claims. No notes text reaches a slide: the out-of-scope line is absent from p. 4, the Question and Runs bullets from p. 11, the four result bullets from p. 12, the five index bullets from p. 13. All of them print in `output/document.pdf` (pp. 2, 3, 5) and in `_site/index.html`.
- Presentation: § What changed, and what did not (p. 12) is the best frame in the deck. One table, one sentence, and the sentence is the finding. It reads in the time a slide is on screen.
- Presentation and website: three content headings state their claim rather than name a topic: "Layers: order is documented, conflict is not", "On demand: reachable is not loaded", "CLAUDE.md as an index, not a manual". Each works as a slide title, a sidebar entry and a landing point from search.
- All three registers: § CLAUDE.md as an index shows the artefact itself, six lines of the file that was run, rather than describing it. The excerpt holds its line breaks on the slide (p. 13), in the document (p. 5) and in the site's `<pre>`.
- Document and website: the four capabilities open and close the tutorial in the same words, and § What you can do now maps each to the unit that delivered it.
- Website (read as source, see Weaknesses): two-level sidebar contents, a table that scrolls inside itself, and both PDFs linked from the header.

## Weaknesses
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What `/context` shows at launch — the figure is not legible at projection size. On p. 10 it occupies about 172 pt of the 453 pt frame width; its row labels come out at roughly 5 pt against 11 pt body text, and the USAGE column is grey on near-black. The source, `figures/context-at-launch.png`, is 441 x 408 px: about 97 ppi at the width the document prints it (document p. 4), and upscaled on the website, where `width:70%` of the main column is wider than the image. The spine's open question, whether the panel survives projection and PDF, has an answer: it does not.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What `/context` shows at launch — the figure is never read. No sentence in any prose file refers to it, and the number it exists to show, 32.3k tokens at launch, appears nowhere in rendered text, only in pixels. The caption says where the capture was taken, not what to see in it. Three of its rows (System tools, the largest at 17.4k; MCP tools; Autocompact buffer) are named nowhere in the document, and the sentence on the frame before it (p. 9) lists instruction files, auto memory and skill descriptions as what loads at launch, which the figure shows to be 9.0k of the 32.3k. Capability 1, "say what each part costs", rests on this figure alone.
- `sections/03-content.concepts.md` § The window is a budget you can read — the spine decides on an "annotated `/context` reading". What shipped is an unannotated crop. Promise is not delivery.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What `/context` shows at launch — in the document the unit has come apart. Heading 3.2 on p. 3 is followed only by the notes sentence "Memory files is this repository's `CLAUDE.md` (4.2k)…", a gloss on a row the reader has not yet seen. The figure floats to p. 4 and sits alone on the page.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What changed, and what did not — in the document, heading 3.4 is the last line of p. 3, stranded above blank space. Its table opens p. 5, a full page later, with the figure page between them.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § On demand: reachable is not loaded — the tutorial defines its subject as what goes in "and when", and no figure in it has a time axis. Three classes of mechanism, the launch boundary and the compaction boundary are carried as bullets across pp. 9, 14 and 15. This is the idea that most needs a figure (Actionable 2).
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § The demo: one prompt, two windows — the frame (p. 11) does not stand alone. The Question is in a notes block, so the slide opens on "Substrate" and never says what the demo asks. The heading says two windows and the frame lists three arms. The Observables bullet is a seven-item list flattened into one sentence of four lines.
- `sections/03-content.concepts.md` § The demo: one prompt, two windows — the spine is stale against the prose. It says "This unit compares A with B. C is reported under § CLAUDE.md as an index"; the prose reports C in the table under § What changed. The heading "two windows" rests on the stale decision.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § CLAUDE.md as an index, not a manual — the frame (p. 13) never says what an index is. The definition (commands, conventions that cannot be inferred, pointers) is in notes; the bullet that is on the slide gives provenance, "six of its 32 lines; subheadings omitted". The audience sees six lines and must infer the rule that chose them.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § The window is a budget you can read — the first paragraph (p. 9) refers to "the manual", "the six behaviours we scored" and "the argument for an index". The demo arrives two frames later and the index four. The unit needs memory of slides that have not been shown, and the same holds for a reader who lands on it from the sidebar.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What changed, and what did not — the table leaves the pattern to arithmetic. Tool calls separate A from B and C; tokens separate C from A and B. The reader has to compare six ranges by eye to see it, and the first two columns are constant across all three rows.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — tight frames, all holding: § How a window fails (p. 7, 11 body lines), § The window is a budget you can read (p. 9, three paragraphs), § The demo (p. 11), § Layers (p. 14), § On demand (p. 15). Each is within about two lines of what the frame carries. Any addition to one of them must be paid for on the same frame.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — from § 3.5 onward the document is the deck with notes attached. Units 3.5 to 3.8 are bullet lists with no sentence leading from one to the next (document pp. 5–6); nothing says why layers follow the index, or on-demand loading follows layers. The notes blocks carry detail and no transitions.
- `sections/02-motivation.prose.md` § How a window fails — the notes sentence about the demo's control prints after the last bullet (document p. 3, top), four bullets away from "Too little", which it supports.
- `sections/04-conclusion.prose.md` and `sections/03-content.prose.md` — cross-references are plain text in all three outputs, and their names do not match the headings they point to ("§ Layers", "§ On demand", "§ The demo"). The document numbers its sections and the references do not use the numbers; the website has anchors and the references do not link to them.
- `references.bib` — six documentation entries share an author and have no date, so they render as "Anthropic, n.d.-a" to "n.d.-f". The labels carry no information, and the deck suppresses the bibliography, so on a slide they resolve to nothing. § Layers (p. 14) spends about a line and a half on five of them.
- `site/style.css` — the header is `position: sticky` and no heading has `scroll-margin-top`, so a reader arriving at `#the-demo-one-prompt-two-windows` has the heading they landed on covered by the bar. Read from the stylesheet, not confirmed in a browser: the sandbox blocked the browser, so the website was reviewed as `_site/index.html` and `site/style.css` source.
- `sections/01-context.prose.md` — the section title "Context" follows a title slide reading "Context Engineering & the Context Window" (pp. 1–2), and heads the sidebar. In a tutorial about context, the divider reads as the subject, not as background. "Content" is as generic in the sidebar.

Callout one brackets the header, 32.3k used before any prompt. Callout two brackets Memory files and Skills, 9.0k, the rows this tutorial teaches the reader to change. Callout three marks the Autocompact buffer, 33.0k, reserved and not counted in the 32.3k. Make the figure wider than tall so that at the filter's 70% height it fills the frame width. Rewrite the caption to state the reading in at most two slide lines, and open the notes block with a sentence that refers to the figure. Then check the rebuilt document: the figure should sit under heading 3.2, and heading 3.4 should not be the last line of a page; if the float still leaves, pin it in the `doc` recipe of the `justfile`, where settings that differ by output belong.
   (*why:* presentation, where it cannot be read; document, where it has left its section; website, where it is upscaled and the key number is not text)
2. `sections/03-content.prose.md` § On demand: reachable is not loaded — add the timeline figure the spine asks about. One horizontal axis, session time, with three marked events: launch, a prompt or tool call, compaction. One lane per mechanism, a bar from the moment it starts to cost tokens. Lanes that start at launch: the `CLAUDE.md` chain with its `@path` imports, skill descriptions. Lanes that start at a trigger, each with an arrow from the trigger: `@file` typed in a prompt, the agent's own read, a subdirectory `CLAUDE.md`, a skill's body. One lane drawn as an empty dashed outline: the `CLAUDE.md` of a directory added with `--add-dir`, reachable and never loaded. At compaction, the project-root `CLAUDE.md` bar restarts and the chat-instruction bar ends. The relationship it must show is when each thing begins to spend budget and whether it survives compaction. On the slide the figure replaces the Front-loaded, On demand and Reachable only bullets, which move to a notes block; Rule and Here stay. No new `##`.
   (*why:* all three; it answers the spine's question, since a time axis and a compaction boundary are not in the two lists)
