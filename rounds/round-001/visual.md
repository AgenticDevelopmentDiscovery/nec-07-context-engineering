**Recommendation:** needs revision
the tutorial's central idea, *when* something enters the window, has no figure; the one figure that exists is an unannotated 441-pixel screenshot the prose never points to; and by estimate most `##` units sit at or over what one slide holds.

## Slide overflow

All entries are estimates made from the prose, not from the rendered deck. Method: each unit wrapped at about 74 characters per line with citations expanded to their rendered form ("(Anthropic, n.d.-d)"), read against roughly 13 to 14 body lines on a 16:9 Metropolis frame at the default size. Confirm each against the page.

- `sections/01-context.prose.md` § What this tutorial covers — likely overflow, about 15 lines: too many ideas. Three things share the frame: the four capabilities, the route ("The route: a readable budget, a demo on this repository, ..."), and the scope line ("Out of scope: prompt wording (Tutorial 06), ..."). The spine already names it an overflow candidate.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § The window is a budget you can read — likely overflow, about 13 lines plus two paragraph gaps: too many ideas. Model (finite budget), instrument (`/context`), and boundary (compaction) are three ideas. The spine names the fallback itself: the compaction lines move to § Pitfalls.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § CLAUDE.md as an index, not a manual — likely overflow, about 13 lines in five bullets of two to three lines each: too many ideas. Cost per session, what an index holds, the sufficient-statistic sentence, the documentation's 200-line target with its delivery mechanism and the template's 240 lines, and the Arm C result. The last is a demo result, not part of the index idea.
- `sections/01-context.prose.md` § What context engineering is — near miss, 12 lines of continuous prose in two paragraphs: genuinely dense. Two ideas (two stores; the definition), both needed, but written as document paragraphs rather than as something projected.
- `sections/02-motivation.prose.md` § How a window fails — near miss, about 13 lines: too many ideas. Five labelled bullets; "Caveat" and "The bound" are separate ideas from the three failure modes. The spine's fallback (the bound moves to § Open edges) applies.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § Layers: order is documented, conflict is not — near miss, about 13 lines: padded. Four of five bullets end in the same citation, "(Anthropic, n.d.-d)", which costs roughly a line and a half on the frame.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § On demand: reachable is not loaded — near miss, about 13 lines: genuinely dense. The content is one three-way contrast; the citation strings ("(Anthropic, n.d.-d, n.d.-c, n.d.-b)") take about two of the lines.
- `sections/04-conclusion.prose.md` § What you can do now — near miss, about 13 lines plus a gap: padded. The parenthetical cross-references ("(§ The demo; § What changed; § CLAUDE.md as an index)") add about three lines to a list the audience has already seen on the second slide.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What `/context` shows at launch — near miss: genuinely dense. `filters/slide-figure-height.lua` sets the image to 75% of the text height; a frame title and a two-sentence caption must fit in what remains.

## Strengths

- Presentation and website: several headings state the claim rather than the topic. "Layers: order is documented, conflict is not" and "On demand: reachable is not loaded" can be read alone on a projected frame or in the sidebar and still say what the unit argues.
- Presentation: the bold lead labels give every bullet an entry point. § On demand is the best case: "Front-loaded", "On demand", "Reachable only", "Rule", "Here" are parallel, so the three-way contrast is visible in the labels before any sentence is read.
- All three registers: the figure is a real reading of this repository rather than a schematic, and it sits under its own `##`, which is the right split for the deck. The caption decodes one row that would otherwise be opaque ("Memory files is this repository's `CLAUDE.md` (4.2k) and the auto-memory index (163)").
- Presentation: the four capabilities are repeated word for word in § What this tutorial covers and § What you can do now, so the closing slide needs no memory of the opening one.
- All three registers: the closing line of § Pitfalls, "Every fix changes what is loaded, or when. None is a rewording.", works as a slide footer, a paragraph end, and a line a search visitor can land on.
- The spines flag their own overflow candidates (01 § What this tutorial covers, 02 § failure modes, 03 § budget and § index). The estimates above agree with all four.

## Weaknesses

- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — Coverage. The single idea that most needs a diagram and has none is *when content crosses into the window*. The subtitle promises "deciding what that is, and when"; § On demand carries it as three bullets of mechanism names, § Layers as a load order in a sentence, § The window is a budget as a paragraph on compaction. The existing figure is a snapshot of one instant (launch), so the time axis the tutorial is about is never drawn. The spine's own open question ("Figure: a timeline of what enters when. Earns its place, or restates the two lists?") has an answer: it earns its place, because the lists cannot show the boundary between loaded and reachable, nor what comes back after compaction.
- `figures/context-at-launch.png` — Teaching value. The image is nine rows of numbers; its only relational content is the usage bar, in which 3% is a few pixels wide. The spine decision calls for an "annotated `/context` reading"; the figure carries no annotation. Nothing tells the reader what to notice: that 32.3k is spent before anything is typed, that "System tools" at 17.4k is over half of that, or that the "Autocompact buffer" row (33.0k) is reserved on top of the 3% shown as used.
- `figures/context-at-launch.png` — The figure works against the slide before it unless the caption frames it. § The window is a budget argues scarcity; the figure shows "Free space 934.7k 93.5%". A projected audience will read the largest number on the screen first.
- `figures/context-at-launch.png` — Resolution and theme. The file is 441 by 408 pixels. At `width=70%` of a 6.5-inch text block that is about 97 dpi in the document; on the website the same attribute stretches it past its native size; on the deck it is scaled up to 75% of the frame height. It is a dark-theme capture with grey text on black (the "USAGE" column), placed on a white page and a light Metropolis frame.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § The window is a budget you can read — Prose and figure disagree. The prose says "`/context` draws current usage as a grid"; the figure shows a bar and a table in a panel with a close button, not a grid. The prose names "instruction files, auto memory and skill descriptions" as the launch load; the figure's two largest rows, "System tools" and "Autocompact buffer", are named nowhere in the prose.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What `/context` shows at launch — Integration. The figure has no identifier and no prose refers to it. The preceding unit ends on compaction, not on a pointer to the reading. In the document the heading becomes a numbered subsection holding only a float, which LaTeX may move away from its heading; on the website it is a heading followed by an image and nothing to read.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What changed, and what did not; § CLAUDE.md as an index — The demo's one positive result is never seen whole. Arms A and B are reported on one slide as inline numbers ("47.2k (44.9–50.3) against 46.6k (44.7–49.1)"), Arm C two slides later ("41.3k tokens (39.9–43.1), its worst run below the best of either other arm"). The comparison the sentence asserts requires the audience to remember two ranges from an earlier frame. Presentation register fails here; the document and website only make it laborious.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § What changed, and what did not — Does not stand alone. "Observables 1 to 5" and "the control" refer to a list on the previous slide that is unnumbered and written as one sentence, and the control is never said to be anything: § The demo lists only "one control", and § How a window fails says only "a convention written nowhere". The heading itself does not say what changed; a website reader arriving from search cannot tell this unit reports demo results.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` § The demo: one prompt, two windows — Register leak. "Arm C, a 32-line index, is reported on the index slide" names a slide in text that also ships in the document and on the website, where there are no slides.
- `sections/01-context.prose.md`, `sections/03-content.prose.md` — The `#` titles "Context" and "Content" are template labels. They become the top-level sidebar entries on the website and the divider pages in the deck. In a tutorial about the context window, a section titled "Context" reads as the subject rather than as background, and the two words differ by two letters on a projected divider.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md`, `sections/04-conclusion.prose.md` — Citations in the deck point at nothing. The slides are built with `suppress-bibliography=true`, so "(Anthropic, n.d.-d)" appears on the frame with no list behind it, and the letter suffixes are opaque in every register: a reader cannot tell that "-d" is the memory page and "-c" the context-window page without turning to the references.
- `sections/03-content.prose.md` — Document register. From § The demo onward the section is bullets only, with no sentence carrying the reader from one unit to the next: nothing says why a demo follows the budget, or why the index follows a null result. The document reads as the deck printed. `topic.md` leaves this open ("decide between `::: notes` blocks ... and accepting a lean document"); it is still undecided with the prose now written.
- `sections/03-content.concepts.md`, `sections/02-motivation.concepts.md`, `sections/01-context.concepts.md` — Spine and prose have drifted. The 03 spine says "Seven slides" and "Every `##` below is one `##` of the prose", but has no unit for § What `/context` shows at launch, so the deck has 14 content slides against a house budget of 13. The 02 spine's heading is "Two ways a window fails"; the prose has "How a window fails". The 01 and 03 spines say "Not run: no `/context` reading is recorded", while a reading is now the figure. The 03 spine decides "Percentages, not token counts"; the caption quotes token counts.
